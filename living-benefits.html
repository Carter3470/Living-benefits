The Key Difference

Living Benefits vs.
Traditional Long-Term Care

❌ Traditional LTC Plans

  • Pays facilities directly — not you
  • Limited to approved care providers
  • You have no control over spending
  • Family caregivers often go unpaid
  • Living and household costs not covered

✅ Living Benefit Plans

  • Money paid to you or your representative
  • You choose who provides your care
  • Full control over how every dollar is used
  • Family caregivers can be compensated
  • Covers living expenses and household costs too
